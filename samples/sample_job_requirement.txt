BACKEND ENGINEER - SAMPLE JOB REQUIREMENT

Company: Example Technology Labs
Location: Jakarta / Hybrid
Employment: Full-time

ROLE SUMMARY
We are looking for a Backend Engineer to build and maintain reliable APIs and
backend services for our SaaS platform.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
1. At least 3 years of professional backend software development experience.
2. Strong Python programming experience.
3. Production experience with FastAPI or a comparable Python web framework.
4. Strong PostgreSQL and relational database experience.
5. Experience designing and consuming REST APIs.
6. Experience using Docker for local development or deployment.
7. Practical AWS experience, especially services such as EC2, S3, RDS, or Lambda.
8. Experience writing automated tests with pytest or an equivalent framework.
9. Good communication and ability to work with product and frontend teams.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
10. Kubernetes experience.
11. CI/CD experience with GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or a similar platform.
12. Experience with Redis and asynchronous background jobs.
13. Familiarity with observability, logging, and metrics.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design, implement, test, and maintain backend services.
- Build secure and well-documented REST APIs.
- Work with PostgreSQL and improve query performance.
- Containerize services with Docker.
- Collaborate with frontend engineers and product stakeholders.
- Review code and participate in technical discussions.

EVALUATION NOTES
Candidates should be assessed based only on job-relevant evidence contained in
the CV. Lack of evidence should not automatically be interpreted as evidence
that a candidate does not have a skill.
